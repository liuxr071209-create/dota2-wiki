## Statistics caveats

Win rates, pick rates, trends, builds, and matchup numbers are descriptive
statistics from public matches. They are not predictions or guarantees.
Positions (1–5) are editorial labels for navigation, not a tournament role
dataset. Small sample sizes can produce volatile percentages.
